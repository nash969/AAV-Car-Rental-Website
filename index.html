Rental Requirements

Submit complete requirements to secure booking.

Your identification and credentials are kept strictly confidential.

  • Driver's License & 1 Primary ID
  • Company ID
  • Proof of Billing
  • Take a selfie holding your ID
  • Reservation fee of ₱500/day
  • QR code from LTO-LTMS portal

The reservation fee is deductible from the total rent amount but will be forfeited if the renter cancels the booking.
